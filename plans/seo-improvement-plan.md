## 🚨 KEY PROBLEMS IDENTIFIED

### 1. CTR Problem on Page 1 Rankings
Pages ranking on page 1 (positions 1-10) are getting ZERO clicks. This means:
- **Title tags** are not compelling enough to click
- **Meta descriptions** are too generic/boring
- **No rich snippets** appearing despite schema markup

### 2. Keyword Cannibalization
Multiple pages compete for the same terms:
- "field service management software" → at least 5 pages target this
- "fsm software" → fsm-software AND fsm-field-service-management
- "mobile field service" → mobile-field-service-management-app AND field-service-management-app

### 3. Content Depth vs. Position
Pages at position 30-50 need MORE topical depth. Google sees them as relevant but not authoritative enough. The content is good (300+ lines) but competitors likely have:
- More internal links
- More specific sub-topics covered
- Better E-E-A-T signals
- More unique data/statistics

### 4. Missing Content Gaps
High-volume queries with NO dedicated page:
- "mobile field service app" (141 impressions) — covered by mobile-field-service-management-app but URL doesn't match
- "field service management app" (245 impressions) — has a page but at position 37
- "service management software for manufacturers" (4 impressions, position 24) — got a CLICK! No dedicated page

### 5. USA Market Problem
6,572 impressions from USA but only 1 click. Average position 50.23 in USA. The content needs stronger USA-market signals.
