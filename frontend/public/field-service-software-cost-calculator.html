How to Choose the Right FSM Software for Your Budget

The most expensive FSM option is not always the best one for your business. Here is how to evaluate field service software based on your actual team size and operational needs:

Under 3 Technicians

For very small teams, entry-level tools like Jobber's Core plan ($49/month) or Workiz's Starter plan may be sufficient. FieldZenPro at $249/month becomes competitive once you reach 3–4 technicians and need features like GPS dispatch and offline capability.

4–10 Technicians

This is where per-seat pricing starts to hurt. A 5-technician Jobber Connect plan runs $249/month — the exact same price as FieldZenPro for unlimited technicians. For 8-10 techs, Workiz and Jobber charge $400-760+/month while FieldZenPro remains $249/month flat. The savings compound significantly over a year.

10–50 Technicians

At this scale, per-seat platforms become prohibitively expensive. ServiceTitan runs $1,250-2,000+/month for a 10-person team. Workiz and Jobber are $400-760/month. FieldZenPro is still $249/month with full GPS dispatch, offline mobile, and QuickBooks sync. Annual savings vs. ServiceTitan: $12,000-21,000+/year.

Key Features to Compare Beyond Price

Price per seat is just one cost factor. Consider these feature-level differences that impact your actual operations:

  • Offline mobile app — essential for areas with poor cell coverage (basements, rural routes, commercial facilities)
  • GPS dispatch board — live technician tracking vs. basic map view
  • QuickBooks sync direction — two-way (automatic) vs. one-way (requires manual reconciliation)
  • Preventive maintenance scheduling — built-in vs. add-on module
  • Setup time — self-service under 2 hours vs. multi-week enterprise onboarding
  • Annual contract — month-to-month vs. forced annual commitment
  • Setup fee — $0 vs. $500-$5,000+ upfront cost
