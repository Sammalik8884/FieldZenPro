What is the main difference in pricing?

Do both software options include dispatching?

Yes, both feature robust dispatching capabilities.

Is QuickBooks Sync available?

Yes, both platforms offer QuickBooks synchronization.

Can I track my technicians via GPS?

FieldZenPro includes comprehensive GPS tracking capabilities natively without extra fees.

Is there a mobile app for field technicians?

Both FieldZenPro and FieldPulse provide fully featured mobile apps.

Are customer communications automated?

Yes, you can automate customer communications in both platforms.
