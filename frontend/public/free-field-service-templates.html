Can I edit these templates?

What is the difference between a work order and an invoice?

A work order documents what work will be done and goes to the technician. An invoice requests payment for completed work and goes to the customer. Both reference the same job number.

When should I switch from templates to FSM software?

When you have 3 or more technicians and spend more than 2 hours/day on scheduling or paperwork. FSM software typically pays for itself within 30-60 days through time savings and faster billing.
