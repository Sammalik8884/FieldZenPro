Must-Have Features in Field Technician Software

Pre-Job Briefing Screen

Before the technician pulls out of the driveway, they should be able to review a complete job briefing: customer name, address with one-tap navigation, reported problem, equipment details, last service date, any special access instructions (gate codes, contact person at commercial sites), and what parts they likely need and whether those parts are on their truck. This 2-minute pre-job review eliminates the majority of on-site surprises.

Structured Digital Work Orders

Work orders should not be blank text fieldsâ€”they should be structured forms that guide the technician through every required step: symptom confirmation, diagnostic findings, parts used, labor time, safety checks completed, and customer communication notes. Structured work orders ensure consistency across your team and capture every data point needed for accurate invoicing and future service reference.

Photo and Video Capture with Annotation

Photos are worth more than a thousand words in field service. The ability to photograph a failing component, annotate the image with arrows and text labels, and attach it directly to the work order serves three purposes: it documents the problem for the customer's understanding and approval, it provides proof of the condition before and after the repair, and it creates a visual record that future technicians can reference.

In-App Parts and Pricing Lookup

A digital price book integrated into the work order means technicians never have to guess a price or call the office for a quote confirmation. Every service, part, and labor rate is available in the app with search and filter functionality. When a technician selects a part from the price book, it is automatically added to both the work order and the invoiceâ€”eliminating the "forgotten part" invoicing gap that costs service businesses thousands per month.
