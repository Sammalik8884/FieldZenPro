FieldZenPro's weather rescheduling tool lets you mass-cancel and reschedule weather-affected jobs in minutes. Select all jobs marked as weather-dependent on a given date with one click. High-Rise and Rope Access Safety Documentation

High-rise window cleaning involving rope access, suspended scaffolding, or aerial lift equipment carries significant safety and liability requirements. OSHA 1926.502 governs fall protection for work at heights. SPRAT (Society of Professional Rope Access Technicians) and IRATA (Industrial Rope Access Trade Association) certifications are industry standards for rope access technicians. Many commercial clients and property managers now require proof of rope access certification before allowing high-rise work on their buildings.

FieldZenPro stores SPRAT and IRATA certification levels (Technician, Supervisor, Rescue) per crew member with expiry dates and automatic renewal alerts. High-rise jobs require completion of a pre-job safety checklist in the mobile app before work can be started: anchor point inspection, harness inspection, rope and equipment check, weather conditions assessment, and crew safety briefing sign-off. All safety documentation stores with the job record and is accessible for OSHA inspection or customer compliance audits.

🏆

SPRAT and IRATA Certification Tracking

Store SPRAT and IRATA certification level, certification number, and expiry date per crew member. High-rise jobs only display certified crew in the assignment picker. Certification expiry alerts fire 90 and 30 days before renewal deadline. Expired certifications remove crew members from high-rise job eligibility automatically.

Pre-Job Safety Checklist

High-rise jobs require digital safety checklist completion before work begins in the app: anchor point inspection, harness condition check, rope and descender inspection, equipment bag check, weather conditions (wind speed, temperature, precipitation), and crew safety briefing acknowledgment. Per-Pane and Per-Square-Foot Billing

Window cleaning pricing structures vary widely: per-pane for residential, per-square-foot for commercial interiors, per-linear-foot for storefront glass, or flat-rate per building for high-rise contracts. When you manage 200 commercial accounts each with a different billing structure, applying the right rate to each job without a system built to handle it creates billing errors, undercharging, and time wasted on manual price calculation.

FieldZenPro supports all window cleaning billing structures configured per customer account. Each account stores its billing method (per-pane, per-sq-ft, per-linear-ft, flat-rate) and the agreed rate. When a job generates for that account, the system applies the correct rate and calculates the invoice total automatically. For accounts billed per pane, the work order prompts technicians to record the pane count on completion. The count rolls into the invoice calculation without dispatcher or office intervention.
