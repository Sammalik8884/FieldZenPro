About FieldZenPro

Our Story

FieldZenPro was founded by a team with deep roots in field service operations. We watched HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and landscaping companies manage critical operations through messy spreadsheets, disconnected apps, and outdated desktop software — and we knew there had to be a better way.

The platforms that existed were either too expensive — ServiceTitan starts at $125+/user/month, pricing out most small and mid-size service companies — or too limited, like Jobber, which lacks a real GPS dispatch board and live technician tracking. We decided to build the platform we wished existed: one that gives a 5-technician plumbing company the same dispatch visibility and job management that a 100-technician enterprise uses, at a price that makes real business sense.

The result is FieldZenPro: $249/month flat for unlimited technicians, with no per-seat fees, no annual contract, and no setup costs.

Our Mission

To give every field service company — regardless of size — the operational tools and clarity they need to deliver exceptional service, dispatch efficiently, and get paid faster. We believe powerful field service management software should not be locked behind enterprise pricing.

What FieldZenPro Does

FieldZenPro is a complete field service management platform used by HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, appliance repair, security, and commercial cleaning companies across the United States.

  • Live GPS Dispatch Board — drag-and-drop scheduling with real-time technician locations
  • Offline Mobile App — iOS and Android, works without internet, syncs automatically
  • Digital Work Orders — photo capture, customer signatures, job notes in the field
  • Invoicing — flat-rate and time-and-materials, send from the field or office
  • QuickBooks Sync — two-way sync with QuickBooks Online, eliminates double entry
  • Customer History — full service history, equipment records, recurring jobs
  • Preventive Maintenance — schedule recurring PM jobs automatically
  • Inventory Management — parts tracking, truck stock, purchase orders

How We Compare to Alternatives

Service companies evaluating FieldZenPro typically compare it against three alternatives. Here is an honest breakdown:

  • ServiceTitan — Powerful, but starts at $125-200+/user/month with annual contracts, setup fees often exceeding $5,000, and a 6-12 month implementation timeline. Designed for companies with 50+ technicians and a dedicated office staff to manage it. Most 5-20 technician companies find it more platform than they need at 5-10x the price.
  • Jobber — Good entry-level scheduling and invoicing tool, but lacks a real GPS live dispatch board, has no offline mobile app, and QuickBooks sync is one-directional. Works well for very small companies but hits limits fast as teams grow past 5 technicians.
  • Spreadsheets + QuickBooks — The most common starting point. Works until it doesn't — missed jobs, no technician visibility, hours of weekly data re-entry, and zero ability to see your operation in real time. Most companies reach breaking point around 3-5 technicians.

FieldZenPro sits in the gap: enterprise-grade GPS dispatch, offline mobile app, and full QuickBooks sync at $249/month flat — for companies that have outgrown spreadsheets but don't need ServiceTitan's complexity or price.
