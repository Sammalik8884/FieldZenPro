Equipment Asset Tracking: Warranty Management Across Hundreds of Installed Systems

A security camera installation company that has been operating for 3-5 years has hundreds of installed systems across commercial and residential customers — each with cameras, NVRs, switches, and cabling installed at a specific date, under a specific equipment warranty (typically 2-3 years for cameras, 1-2 years for NVRs). When a customer calls reporting a camera failure, the support technician needs to know immediately: is this camera under manufacturer warranty? What model was installed? When was it installed? Who installed it? Without an asset tracking system, answering these questions means searching through old paper proposals or QuickBooks invoices — a 10-15 minute exercise per ticket that erodes support margin.

FieldZenPro's equipment asset module records every installed component at the job level: camera model and serial number, NVR model, firmware version at time of installation, warranty expiration date, and installation technician. When a service call comes in for a customer property, the technician opens the site record and sees the complete installed equipment history before arriving on site — camera serial numbers for RMA processing, the NVR admin credentials stored securely in the site notes, the cable routing map from the original installation photos. Warranty tracking alerts the service team 60 days before equipment warranties expire, enabling proactive extended warranty upsell conversations before the customer discovers an out-of-warranty failure at the worst possible time.
