Why HVAC Dispatch Is Different From Every Other Trade

The dispatching challenges that make HVAC operationally distinct from other field service trades are structural, not just volumetric. Seasonal demand volatility: No other trade experiences the demand swing that HVAC does. The first 95-degree day of summer generates 3â€“5 times the call volume of a typical spring day. EPA 608 compliance requirement: HVAC is the only residential trade with a mandatory federal environmental compliance requirement for a routine service activity. Residential vs commercial operational split: Most HVAC businesses serve both residential and commercial customers, but the dispatch requirements for each are fundamentally different. Equipment complexity and specialisation: HVAC equipment ranges from simple residential split systems to complex commercial chillers, VRF systems, and building automation controllers. A technician qualified for residential equipment may not be qualified for commercial rooftop units.
