The Pricing Reality: What You Actually Pay at Scale

The most concrete reason to evaluate a Workiz alternative is pricing. Workiz's per-seat model is economical at 1–3 technicians, but the monthly cost grows linearly with every technician you add — creating a scaling penalty that is directly opposite to the unit economics of a growing service business. As you grow your technician team, your revenue per technician typically improves (better route density, better parts availability, more efficient dispatching), but your Workiz cost per month rises proportionally with headcount, not with revenue. FieldZenPro's $249/month flat rate means that adding your 6th, 8th, or 12th technician costs $0 in additional software fees — the platform scales with your business without penalising growth.

How to Switch from Workiz to FieldZenPro

Switching field service management platforms is a project that most businesses are reasonably anxious about — customer data, job history, price books, equipment records, and QuickBooks mapping are all at stake. FieldZenPro's migration process is designed to eliminate the most anxiety-inducing elements of the transition: data loss, configuration complexity, and the disruption of having an unconfigured new platform in production during a busy week. The process typically follows five steps over a 5–7 business day period.

Step one is data export from Workiz — your customer list, job history, price book, and any equipment records are exported in Workiz's standard export format and sent to the FieldZenPro migration team. Step two is the FieldZenPro configuration session with your dedicated onboarding specialist — your trade, service types, billing modes, and QuickBooks account structure are configured before any data is imported. Step three is data import — your Workiz export is imported into FieldZenPro's configured environment. Step four is the parallel run period — most businesses run FieldZenPro and Workiz simultaneously for 5–7 business days, entering new jobs in FieldZenPro while completing any Workiz jobs already in progress. Step five is the cutover — Workiz is discontinued, and all job management moves to FieldZenPro. Your FieldZenPro onboarding specialist remains available throughout the transition and for the first 30 days after cutover for any configuration questions.
