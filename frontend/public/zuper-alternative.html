What Is Zuper and Why Do Companies Look for Alternatives?

Zuper is a field service management platform launched in 2019, targeting mid-to-large enterprises. It offers scheduling, work order management, customer communication tools, and analytics. It integrates with platforms like Salesforce and ServiceNow — which tells you exactly who it is built for.

The problem is that most field service businesses are not Salesforce customers. They are HVAC contractors with 5 technicians. They are electrical contractors with 12 vans. They are plumbing companies with 8 employees. For these businesses, Zuper's enterprise feature set creates complexity and cost that provides no return.

The most common complaints from Zuper users who switch away:

  • Per-seat pricing that grows with your team — at $35–$75 per user per month, adding a dispatcher or an office admin costs real money every month
  • Complex onboarding — Zuper's enterprise orientation means setup is measured in weeks, not hours
  • Features built for enterprise workflows — field service businesses with 5–50 technicians pay for complexity they never use
  • Support tiers — smaller customers report slower response times compared to enterprise accounts

The Pricing Problem: Why Zuper Gets Expensive Fast

How the Migration from Zuper Works

Switching FSM platforms is the most common reason service companies don't switch — they are afraid of disruption. FieldZenPro's migration process is designed to eliminate that fear with a structured 5-step process:

  1. Data export from Zuper — Our team provides a checklist of exactly what to export: customer records, job history, equipment records, price books, and technician accounts.
  2. FieldZenPro account configuration — We configure your service types, territory zones, technician skills, and custom job statuses to match your operation.
  3. Data import and validation — All records are imported and verified. You review a sample before we proceed.
  4. Parallel operation (5–7 days) — Your team runs both systems simultaneously. New jobs go into FieldZenPro; Zuper serves as historical reference.
  5. Full cutover — Zuper is decommissioned. FieldZenPro becomes your sole operational platform.

Most companies complete this process in 48–72 hours of actual working time spread across 5–7 business days.
