Snow removal is operationally unlike any other field service business. You don't schedule jobs — a storm schedules them for you, at 3am, when it's -15F, with 6 hours of notice. Your dispatch has to go from zero to full activation across 50 properties in under an hour. Your billing depends on how much salt you applied at each property, which your operators have to track accurately while running routes in the dark. And every slip-and-fall claim starts with the question: "Can you prove your crew serviced that parking lot before the incident?"

Generic field service software can't handle any of this effectively. This guide covers what snow removal software specifically needs to do — trigger dispatch, material tracking, liability documentation, subcontractor management — and how FieldZenPro addresses each.

Photo Documentation for Slip-and-Fall Liability Defense

Slip-and-fall liability is the most significant legal risk in snow removal. Claims can arrive months after the incident, and without contemporaneous documentation of your service, your defense relies entirely on your word against the claimant's. FieldZenPro's photo documentation turns every service visit into a timestamped evidence record:

📸

Timestamped, GPS-Tagged Photos

Every photo taken from the mobile app carries the exact timestamp and GPS coordinates — independent metadata that cannot be altered after the fact, unlike an undated photo on a personal phone.

🗺️

Before and After Service Photos

Operators photograph conditions on arrival (snow depth, ice visibility) and after service (cleared pavement, salt coverage). This establishes the state of the property before and after your crew was there.

⚠️

Pre-Existing Hazard Documentation

If an operator notices a hazard they can't address — a broken drain grate, a pothole under snow, an icy area that requires the client's facilities team — they photograph and flag it. The property owner notification is automatic.

📋

Service Log PDF Export

Any job can be exported as a PDF service report: arrival time, completion time, materials applied, photos, and operator signature — ready to share with your insurer or legal team within minutes of a claim.

🔍

Searchable by Date and Property

When a claim arrives 3 months after the incident with a specific date, you can retrieve the complete service record for that property and date in under 60 seconds — not a manual search through filing cabinets.

📅

Storm Event Timeline

Each storm event creates a timeline of all service activity — when each property was first serviced, when salt was applied, when re-checks were done — giving you a complete chronological defense record for the entire event.

Subcontractor Management During Major Storm Events

Major storm events — the ones that drop 12+ inches — often exceed your own crew capacity. The properties that need plowing at 4am aren't going to wait for your 3 operators to finish their routes and start again. Subcontractors fill the gap, but managing them alongside your own crew without a system creates billing gaps and quality gaps.

FieldZenPro supports subcontractor operations as integrated field team members:

  • Subcontractor profiles: Each sub has their own login, assigned equipment (truck type, plow size, spreader capacity), and service zone or property list. Their rates and billing terms are stored in their profile
  • Route assignment during activation: When you activate storm routes, you can assign specific properties or route segments to subcontractors directly from the same dispatch board you use for your own crew — no separate system or separate phone calls
  • Same documentation requirements: Subcontractors use the same mobile app to log service arrival, capture photos, record material usage, and mark properties complete. Their work records integrate into your master storm event record — you have documentation for every property regardless of whether a staff operator or subcontractor serviced it
  • Subcontractor billing vs client billing: FieldZenPro tracks what each subcontractor performed and what their agreed rates are — generating a subcontractor payment summary at storm close while separately generating client invoices at your contracted rate
