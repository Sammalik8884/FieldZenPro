Setting Up Your SLA Tiers

FieldZenPro lets you define custom SLA response windows that match your contracts and service agreements:

  • P1 Emergency: Technician dispatched within 30 minutes, on-site within 2 hours (typical for commercial service agreements)
  • P2 Urgent: Dispatched within 2 hours, on-site within 4 hours
  • P3 Standard: Scheduled within 24 hours, on-site within 48 hours or at the agreed appointment window
  • Planned Maintenance: Pre-scheduled visits tied to maintenance contract calendar — no SLA urgency but tracked against contract completion rates

Skill-Based Assignment: Matching Every Job to the Right Technician

Proximity is not the only factor in dispatch quality. Sending the closest technician who doesn't have the right certification for a job creates a callback — the technician cannot complete the work, the customer is frustrated, and you're paying for two visits on one job's revenue.

FieldZenPro stores each technician's certified skills, equipment qualifications, and service zone as part of their profile. When a dispatcher creates a new job, they tag the required skills for that job type. The assignment panel then filters the technician list to show only those with matching skills — along with their current GPS proximity and workload level.

Skill Profile Example: HVAC Technician

  • EPA 608 Certification: Type I (Small Appliances), Type II (High-Pressure), Universal — stored with expiry date
  • Equipment Qualifications: Carrier systems, Trane residential, commercial chiller systems, rooftop units (RTU)
  • Service Zones: Downtown district, North suburbs (excludes industrial zone)
  • License: State HVAC contractor license #HC-28441, expires March 2027
  • Languages: English, Spanish (useful for customer communication filtering)

When a commercial chiller emergency comes in — which requires EPA Universal certification and chiller system qualification — the dispatch filter surfaces only the 3 technicians (out of 12) who meet both requirements, ordered by proximity and current workload. The dispatcher picks the best fit in one click instead of calling around to find out who's qualified.

Certification Expiry Protection: When a technician's EPA certification or trade license expires, FieldZenPro automatically removes that skill from their profile. They will no longer appear in filtered dispatch results for jobs requiring that certification — protecting your business from liability and ensuring compliance without manual monitoring.

Route Optimization: Sequencing the Day for Minimum Drive Time

FieldZenPro's route optimization does two things: it sequences a technician's existing job list in the most efficient geographic order, and it identifies gaps in a technician's day where nearby unscheduled jobs can be inserted without disrupting confirmed appointments.

How the Optimization Runs

Each morning, FieldZenPro calculates the optimal job sequence for each technician by factoring:

  • Fixed appointment windows: Jobs with confirmed arrival windows anchor the sequence around them
  • Job duration estimates: Each job type has a configured average duration — a preventive maintenance visit is 45 min, a full installation is 3 hours — so the routing accounts for time-on-site, not just travel time
  • Start and end location: Routes begin from the technician's home address (morning first dispatch) or current GPS location (mid-day re-routing)
  • Live traffic: Route timing updates throughout the day as traffic conditions change — if a technician falls behind schedule, the dispatcher sees their updated ETAs in real time and can re-sequence if needed
  • Zone preferences: Technicians with defined service zones stay within their area unless emergency override is applied

The optimized route sequence is pushed to the technician's mobile app. They follow job-to-job navigation with one tap on each job completion — no manual address entry, no dispatcher calls to give them the next address.

On-Call Rotation Setup

You define on-call schedules by time window (weekday evenings 5pm–8am, Saturday, Sunday, holidays). FieldZenPro assigns a technician (or on-call tier list) to each window. The on-call schedule is visible to all dispatchers and updates automatically as the week progresses.
