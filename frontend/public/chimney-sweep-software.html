NFPA 211 Level I, II, and III Inspection Documentation

NFPA 211 defines three levels of chimney, fireplace, and venting system inspection with distinct scope requirements. Level I is a visual inspection of accessible portions of the exterior and interior without special tools or equipment -- appropriate for a system in continued use with no changes. Level II is required for real estate transactions, appliance changes, or following any event (chimney fire, earthquake, flooding) that could have damaged the system; it requires video scanning of the flue. Level III is required when hidden areas must be accessed and may involve removal of portions of the building or chimney.

FieldZenPro includes digital inspection forms for all three NFPA 211 levels. Each level captures the appropriate scope fields: appliance type and fuel, flue dimensions, liner type and condition, clearances, exterior condition, obstruction findings, water damage, creosote grading, and pass/fail determination with deficiency notes. Level II inspection records include video file attachment. The completed report generates as a branded PDF at job close, emails to the customer, and can be forwarded to their insurance company or real estate attorney directly from the job record.

Creosote Buildup Stage Grading

Creosote deposits are the primary fire hazard in chimney systems, and their severity varies significantly by stage. Stage 1 deposits are dusty or flaky and can be removed with standard brushing during a sweep. Stage 2 deposits are tar-like, crunchy, or shiny and require rotary cleaning tools; standard sweeping cannot remove them and leaves the system at continued fire risk. Stage 3 deposits are a hardened, concentrated glaze that requires chemical treatment (and sometimes liner replacement) to remove safely; a Stage 3 chimney cannot be safely used until the creosote is fully addressed.

FieldZenPro work orders include a dedicated creosote grading field for each inspection and sweep. Technicians record the observed stage (1, 2, or 3) in the flue and firebox separately. Stage 2 findings automatically generate a recommended rotary cleaning service estimate. Stage 3 findings generate a critical safety advisory to the customer, a do-not-use recommendation on the inspection report, and an urgent follow-up task. Creosote grading history per property tracks whether a customer's usage habits are creating recurring Stage 2 or 3 buildup -- a conversation driver for more frequent sweeping or appliance use guidance.
