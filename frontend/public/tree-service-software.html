Crew and Equipment Scheduling as Linked Resources

Tree service jobs are not assigned to individual technicians -- they are assigned to crews with specific equipment configurations. A large tree removal job requires a crew of four (lead climber, ground crew of two, chipper operator), a bucket truck, and a 12-inch chipper. If the bucket truck is already committed to another job that day, the removal cannot proceed even if the crew is available. Generic scheduling software that treats crew and equipment as separate concerns creates double-booking conflicts that waste crew time and require expensive rescheduling calls to customers.

FieldZenPro links crew members and equipment to job assignments simultaneously. Each job specifies the required equipment (bucket truck, stump grinder, chipper, mini-crane). Equipment availability is checked alongside crew availability in the same scheduler. When a job is assigned, both the crew members and the equipment are blocked from other assignments during the same time window. The dispatch board shows equipment utilization rate alongside crew utilization, giving operations managers a single view of their total fleet capacity.

ISA Certified Arborist Dispatch Filtering

ISA Certified Arborist status is increasingly required by commercial clients, municipalities, and insurance companies for tree risk assessments, hazard evaluations, and certain removal jobs. The certification requires 3 years of professional experience, an exam, and continuing education for renewal every 3 years. Some states require ISA certification or state-equivalent credentials for licensed tree contractors working near utility lines or performing municipal street tree work.

FieldZenPro stores each arborist's ISA certification number, type (ISA Certified Arborist, ISA Board Certified Master Arborist, ISA Certified Arborist Utility Specialist, ISA Certified Tree Worker), and expiry date. Jobs that require a certified arborist filter the dispatch pool to only credentialed crew members. Certification expiry alerts fire 90 days before renewal deadline, with the upcoming renewal linked to continuing education hour requirements.

Storm Damage Emergency Response and Zone Dispatching

A single severe storm event can generate 200 emergency service calls in 24 hours across your service area. Dispatching those jobs individually through normal workflow -- one job at a time, one phone call at a time, one dispatcher managing 200 inbound calls -- is not operationally possible. Tree service companies that handle storm response profitably do so through geographic zone batching: crews work entire zones rather than individually dispatched addresses, and prioritization happens by risk level (tree on structure vs road blockage vs cosmetic damage) rather than call order.

FieldZenPro includes a storm response mode that activates when emergency job volume exceeds your configured threshold. Jobs are automatically grouped by geographic cluster (configurable by radius or by street zone). Priority scores assign automatically based on job type tags: structure impact (Priority 1), road obstruction (Priority 2), utility proximity (Priority 3), standard storm cleanup (Priority 4). Crew leaders see their zone queue in the mobile app and work through it autonomously, checking in photos and job completion without requiring dispatcher contact on each individual job.

Municipal Permit Tracking for Tree Removal

Many municipalities require permits before removing street trees, heritage trees, or trees above a certain diameter on private property within city limits. Some jurisdictions require a certified arborist to submit the permit application. Permit approval can take 2-10 business days. Starting a removal job without the required permit creates stop-work orders, fines, and sometimes required replanting at the contractor's expense -- costs that dwarf the permit fee.

FieldZenPro tracks tree removal permit requirements by jurisdiction. When a removal job is created in a permit-required jurisdiction, the system automatically creates a permit application task before the job can be scheduled. Permit number, submission date, approval date, and approval conditions store on the job record. The job cannot be moved to "scheduled" status until permit approval is confirmed. Permits approaching expiration (many have 60-90 day windows after approval) alert the operations manager to ensure the job is completed before the permit lapses.

🏛

Permit Requirement by Jurisdiction

Configure permit requirements per jurisdiction. Removal jobs in permit-required areas automatically generate a permit application task. Jobs cannot be scheduled until permit approval is recorded. Prevents costly stop-work orders from starting removal without permit in hand.

📅

Permit Approval Tracking

Store permit number, submission date, approval date, issuing authority, and any approval conditions per job. Permit approval date triggers notification to the scheduler that the job is now cleared for scheduling. Conditional approvals (replanting requirements, timing restrictions) display on the job work order.

Job Costing by Service Type: Pruning vs Removal vs Stump vs Planting

Tree service businesses often do not know which services are actually profitable until it is too late to adjust pricing. Pruning looks busy but equipment hours are low. Stump grinding generates strong margins per hour of work. Large removal jobs look profitable on the invoice but consume disproportionate equipment and crew hours when the actual cost of the crane rental, dump fees, and six-person crew is calculated. Without per-service-type job costing, pricing decisions are based on intuition rather than data.

FieldZenPro tracks all costs per job and breaks them out by service type. Labor hours by role (lead climber, ground crew, operator), equipment hours by unit (bucket truck, chipper, stump grinder, crane), disposal and dump fees, and subcontracted services all roll into a job cost total that the system compares against the job's invoiced amount. Profitability reports by service type show which services deserve price increases and which are being sold below true cost.

Debris Removal and Dump Fee Tracking
